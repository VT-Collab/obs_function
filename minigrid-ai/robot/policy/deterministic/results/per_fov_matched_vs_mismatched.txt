==================================================================
HONEST CAVEAT — do not omit this from a paper
==================================================================
The "dynamic beats matched static" pattern is clean and consistent at the
DEFAULT 190-step budget (+2.0 pts at all three true FOVs). At 300+ steps
it still holds for true FOV=120 and 180, but INVERTS for true FOV=60:
dynamic actually underperforms every static variant (including the
mismatched ones) by 2-4 points once the budget is generous. This is a
real, reproducible finding (identical at 500 and 1000), not noise — worth
investigating (likely: FOV=60 gives the inference engine the least
information per step to disambiguate hypotheses, so a larger fraction of
a long episode is spent in cautious/uncertain mode), but should be
reported honestly rather than only showing the 190-step table.
